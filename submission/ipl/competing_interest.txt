Competing interest — what the journal wants, and the text for it.

The live Guide for Authors (read 2026-09-26) says: "The declarations tool should always be
completed", "Authors with no competing interests to declare should select the option 'I have
nothing to declare'", and "The resulting Word document containing your declaration should be
uploaded at the 'attach/upload files' step in the submission process. It is important that the
Word document is saved in the .doc/.docx file format. Author signatures are not required."

So: open Elsevier's declarations tool from the submission, select "I have nothing to declare",
save the .docx it produces, and upload it at the attach-files step. Nothing here is typed by
hand; this file exists so the step is not forgotten.

For reference, the same statement is already in the manuscript, before the references:

    The author declares that he has no known competing financial interests or personal
    relationships that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

Funding, also in the manuscript, in the journal's recommended wording:

    This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public,
    commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.
